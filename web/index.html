FAQ

Things we get asked.

Reach us at hello@plate.best — a real human replies within a day.

01

Is this just MyFitnessPal with AI on top?

No. Plate is built for people training 3-5×/week — the database, the macros, the coach all assume you actually move. The AI is the connective tissue, not the marketing.

02

How accurate is the food scan?

Median match confidence is 94% on home-plated meals. Below 75%, the app says so and asks you to adjust the portion. We'd rather hedge than log a wrong number.

03

What does Coach Kai actually do?

Reads your food log, training history, weight trend, and goals. Suggests meals, calls out gaps, plans your week. Built on Anthropic's Claude — tuned for honest, useful nutrition coaching.

04

Does it work on Android?

Yes — feature-identical on iOS and Android.

05

Can I export my data?

CSV export of every meal, workout, and weight entry, anytime. Your data, your choice.

06

What about privacy?

We don't sell your data and never will. Food photos are stored privately, not shared. Full details on the Privacy page.
