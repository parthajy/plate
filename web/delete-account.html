Account deletion

Delete your Plate account.

Delete your Plate account and everything in it — in the app in under a minute, or by email if you can't sign in. Here's exactly what happens.

Delete in the app

The fastest way — takes under a minute:

  • Open the Plate app and sign in.
  • Tap the You tab (bottom-right).
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  • Confirm when prompted. Your account and data are deleted right away.

Delete by email

If you can't sign in, email hello@plate.best from the address on your Plate account with the subject "Delete my account". We'll verify it's you and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile — body metrics, goals, and sport selections
  • All food logs, workouts, and weight entries
  • Your pantry items and generated recipes
  • Your full conversation history with Coach Kai
  • Food-scan photos stored in our cloud storage

There is no "deactivate" or recovery window — deletion is immediate and permanent. If you want to keep a copy first, export your data from You → Data → Export before deleting.

What's kept, and for how long

  • Active systems: account data is removed immediately, and in all cases within 7 days.
  • Food-scan images: purged from cloud storage within 90 days.
  • Encrypted backups: roll off within 90 days, after which the data is permanently unrecoverable.
  • Subscription & payment records: if you ever bought Plate Pro, Apple and Google keep their own record of that transaction under their policies and applicable tax law. We can't delete records held by Apple or Google.

Contact

Questions about deleting your account? Email hello@plate.best — a real person replies within one business day.
